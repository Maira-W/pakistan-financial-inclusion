Financial Inclusion Gap in Pakistan

Where are people excluded from formal finance, and how much do gender and geography shape that gap? Account ownership is compared with India, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka. Bank branch density is compared across provinces and 119 districts.

Sources: World Bank Global Findex (account ownership, 2011–2024, via the World Bank API); State Bank of Pakistan, Statistics on Scheduled Banks, district-wise branches at 31 Dec 2020; Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Population Census 2023 (district population, from a third-party table conversion).

Read with care: Branch counts show where formal banks are, not where people bank, and they don't include mobile wallets or agents. Branch data is from 2020 and population from 2023. Azad Jammu & Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan are excluded. Low branch density doesn't by itself prove unmet demand.
