Financial inclusion in Pakistan

Who is left out of formal finance, where they live, why, and what it would take to reach them. Built from the World Bank Global Findex, the State Bank of Pakistan's branch register and wallet statistics, and the 2023 census.

The short version

Most adults in Pakistan have no account, and the gap is widest for women, the poorest and rural households. Branches thin out away from the big cities, and four years of new branches did not change that. The digital layer is denser than branches but follows the same map.

    What explains the pattern, and did it change?

    A count model predicts how many branches a district should have given its population, urbanisation and development. Districts well below that line have fewer branches than similar districts. A second test asks whether districts that started with few branches caught up between 2020 and 2024.
